ClinicFrame

Product Hunt: ClinicFrame — Like Granola, but for healthcare. Fully HIPAA-compliant.

Updated 2026-07-29 · Source https://www.producthunt.com/r/DBV5VMI6JYIDKZ?utm_campaign=producthunt- · 中文版

What is the idea?

ClinicFrame is a HIPAA-compliant tool that automates patient documentation and record management for small clinics, offering seamless integration with existing workflows and ensuring compliance without additional overhead.

Who would use it, and what do they get?

A single practitioner — a solo dentist, physiotherapist, counsellor or vet — who types up their own notes after the last patient of the day. Not a hospital IT department, not a procurement committee. What they get back is the evening they currently spend writing notes.

Why would anyone bother?

Unlike generic tools, ClinicFrame is tailored for healthcare providers, focusing specifically on compliance and documentation needs, reducing manual errors and saving time for overburdened staff.

What is missing today?

Current solutions are either too expensive for small clinics or require technical expertise to set up and maintain, leaving a significant portion of the market underserved.

What makes this one different?

Package a simple, affordable, and plug-and-play solution specifically for small clinics that lack IT resources, focusing on ease of use and affordability.

What is the sharpest thing about it?

Ambient note-taking is already proven in ordinary meetings (Granola). The consulting room is the one room where the note is legally load-bearing — which is precisely why the general-purpose tool cannot walk in, and why a purpose-built one has a door of its own.

What could kill it?

Large EHR systems like Epic and Cerner dominate the market and could easily add lightweight compliance features, making standalone solutions redundant.

Who pays, and for what?

Small clinic owners and managers pay to reduce compliance risks and save staff time on documentation, ensuring smooth operations without hiring additional personnel.

What is the main risk?

Major EHR incumbents could integrate similar features, or small clinics may opt for manual processes to avoid additional costs.

How much work is it?

about two months of work
