YouTube Guitar Tab Parser

Show HN: YouTube Guitar Tab Parser

Updated 2026-07-27 · Source https://github.com/marcelpanse/youtube-guitar-tab-parser · 中文版

What is the idea?

Parses a YouTube guitar performance into a tab you can follow along with.

Who would use it, and what do they get?

Someone learning a specific song alone; the deliverable is a tab they can play from tonight.

What is missing today?

Ultimate Guitar is a library of tabs that already exist; if it is not in the library it does not exist. Yet in most lesson videos the tab is right there in the picture and the sound — nobody takes it out.

What makes this one different?

Only do the one video the user already found; hand back a tab that explicitly marks the bars the machine is unsure of, so a friend who plays can fix twelve of them in five minutes instead of transcribing from zero. Never host audio. Cold start (Chinese): 「求谱」 comments under B站 guitar covers; (English): the 'anyone have a tab for this' posts in r/guitarlessons.

What could kill it?

Copyright is the fatal line here: Ultimate Guitar survives on community uploads plus a takedown process, and a lone tool has neither. And the moment a learner hears that the auto-transcription is wrong, they never open it again.

Who pays, and for what?

Self-taught players learning specific songs. Freemium, paid export.

What is the main risk?

Takedowns, plus accuracy that is audible when wrong.

What did real people actually say?

Verbatim from the public sources linked above, not paraphrased: “Some of my favorite songs don't have readily available tabs and I thought to ask, how does one play by ear?” — r/Guitar — 'Music Theory and Playing By Ear', 27 comments

How much work is it?

about two months of work
