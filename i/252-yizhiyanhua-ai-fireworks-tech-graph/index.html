yizhiyanhua-ai/fireworks-tech-graph

yizhiyanhua-ai/fireworks-tech-graph — Generate production-quality SVG+PNG technical diagrams from natural language. 7 styles, UML support, and AI/Agent workfl

Updated 2026-07-28 · Source https://github.com/yizhiyanhua-ai/fireworks-tech-graph · 中文版

What is the idea?

Type a description in natural language, get back a production-quality SVG and PNG technical diagram — not a whiteboard sketch, a figure you can put in front of an audience.

Who would use it, and what do they get?

Strong and it is the one sanctioned exception — AI hands a non-designer a finished artifact they can put straight into a lesson, a deck, or a post.

Why would anyone bother?

Choose fireworks-tech-graph for its ability to generate geometry-checked diagrams that integrate seamlessly with developer workflows, making it ideal for technical documentation and architecture design.

What is missing today?

Mermaid and its kin produce something correct and ugly; design tools produce something beautiful and slow. The gap is one clean, presentable figure produced in the ten seconds a normal person will actually spend, in Chinese as well as English (Mermaid's CJK layout is still bad).

What makes this one different?

Own one audience's figure vocabulary rather than 'any diagram': the middle-school science teacher's worksheet figure, or the CN-market slide diagram with proper CJK typesetting that Mermaid still botches. Cold start CN = 小红书「教师备课」「PPT 配图」标签下的求助帖; EN = r/Teachers and r/PowerPoint 'how do I make this diagram' posts.

What is the sharpest thing about it?

The AI-authored 'Dark Luxury' style offers a unique aesthetic that is not available in competing tools.

What could kill it?

Diagram-from-text is being absorbed into every chat assistant as a default feature, so a standalone tool has maybe a year unless it owns a specific figure style people can recognise and ask for by name.

Who pays, and for what?

Software engineers and technical project managers pay for fireworks-tech-graph to enhance their documentation and diagramming processes, addressing the inefficiencies of manual diagram creation.

What is the main risk?

The reliance on existing diagramming tools like Lucidchart or Microsoft Visio, which have established user bases and comprehensive feature sets, poses a risk to adoption.
