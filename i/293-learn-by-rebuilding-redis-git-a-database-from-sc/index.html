Learn by rebuilding Redis

Show HN: Learn by rebuilding Redis, Git, a database from scratch

Updated 2026-07-29 · Source https://shipthatcode.com · 中文版

What is the idea?

Instead of explaining Redis, it walks you through rebuilding a small one — the understanding is a side effect of having typed it.

Who would use it, and what do they get?

Career-switchers and self-taught learners — non-CS-degree adults; the deliverable is one thing they built and can explain in an interview.

Why would anyone bother?

Users often struggle with existing resources that are too abstract or theoretical, leaving them confused about practical applications; this project offers a tangible, step-by-step learning experience.

What is missing today?

The whole learning market sells watching. Almost nothing sells the scaffolded rebuild — the stage between 'follow along' and 'you are on your own' — and an LLM is the first thing that can hold that scaffold open for one specific learner at their own pace.

What makes this one different?

Sell the stuck-at-11pm moment, not the syllabus: one project, and a reviewer that reads YOUR half-broken version rather than diffing you against a reference solution. Cold start CN = V2EX/掘金「从零实现」系列的评论区与 B站 手撸系列弹幕; EN = r/learnprogramming 'I finished the tutorial and can't build anything' threads (a recurring top post).

What could kill it?

Build-your-own-X lists have existed for a decade and mostly rot as bookmarks; the hard part was never the list, it was somebody catching you at the exact line where you got stuck at 11pm.

Who pays, and for what?

The primary buyers are self-taught developers and coding bootcamp students seeking practical experience in building real-world applications.

What is the main risk?

The dominance of established educational platforms could limit user acquisition and retention for new entrants.

How much work is it?

about two weeks of work
