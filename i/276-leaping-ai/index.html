Leaping AI

Product Hunt: Leaping AI — AI agents that call and text in multi-day campaigns

Updated 2026-07-29 · Source https://www.producthunt.com/r/ZTOYWVYEMJZG4Y?utm_campaign=producthunt… · 中文版

What is the idea?

Voice agents that run an outbound sequence over days rather than a single call: they phone and text a prospect, remember the last conversation, and keep going until the person books or says no.

Who would use it, and what do they get?

Non-developer user: the owner-operator of a small service business. Finished thing: the follow-ups that would have been forgotten, actually sent.

Why would anyone bother?

Choose Leaping AI for its ability to maintain personalized communication over longer periods, which traditional CRM tools lack.

What is missing today?

It is built for a sales team with a CRM. The person who actually loses money to unmade follow-ups is a two-person shop with a notebook, and every product in this category starts by asking which CRM you use.

What makes this one different?

Invert it: do not call the customer, call the owner. The same multi-day machinery, pointed inward — it phones the owner once a day with the three follow-ups that are going cold and drafts the message for each, which the owner sends themselves. No consent problem, no CRM, no dialler. Cold start (Chinese): 小红书 and 抖音 comment sections under 单人理发店 / 修车行 daily-life posts; Cold start (English): r/smallbusiness, where owners describe exactly this failure — 'I sometimes am agreeing to appointment times and days. But then I just keep it all in my head'.

What is the sharpest thing about it?

Leaping AI uniquely combines voice and text communication in a single platform, enabling seamless multi-day engagement.

What could kill it?

Outbound AI calling is the most crowded lane in the whole voice-agent market and it is being regulated in both directions — consent rules in the US and EU, and in China outbound marketing calls are effectively closed to a small operator. The honest risk is not competition, it is that the product is illegal to run at the price point that makes it interesting.

Who pays, and for what?

A one-to-five-person service business, priced against a single recovered job per month.

What is the main risk?

Regulation, not competition: automated outbound calling is where this category dies, and the pivot inward gives up the thing that makes it sound exciting.

What did real people actually say?

Verbatim from the public sources linked above, not paraphrased: “I sometimes am agreeing to appointment times and days. But then I just keep it all in my head … cause I'm in the field with tools in my hands… I got nowhere to put down this appointment info” — reddit

How much work is it?

about two months of work
