SoundGate Guitar

Product Hunt: SoundGate Guitar — AI Music Tutor that gives real-time feedback on your playing

Updated 2026-07-29 · Source https://www.producthunt.com/r/MMIYN6ZQTIOL72?utm_campaign=producthunt… · 中文版

What is the idea?

SoundGate listens through the phone mic while you play and reacts in the moment: which note you actually hit, whether the chord rang clean, whether you drifted off the beat.

Who would use it, and what do they get?

Self-taught adult beginners with a real guitar and no teacher; the deliverable is one sentence per practice session naming the bar they keep missing.

Why would anyone bother?

Users often complain about traditional music lessons being too rigid and lacking instant feedback, which SoundGate Guitar addresses with its real-time analysis.

What is missing today?

Every big guitar app (Yousician, Simply Guitar, Fender Play) sells a curriculum and grades you against ITS songs. Nobody grades you against the song you already chose to learn — the one you looked up a tab for last night. The listening layer is finally cheap enough to be decoupled from the course.

What makes this one different?

Don't ship a course. Ship the ear: point it at any tab or YouTube lesson the learner already has open, and just tell them which bar they keep fluffing. Cold start CN = 小红书/B站 '吉他自学' 打卡帖下面的评论区 (人们在那里贴自己的录音求点评); EN = r/guitarlessons weekly 'critique my playing' threads.

What is the sharpest thing about it?

It grades you against the song you already chose to learn — the tab you looked up last night — instead of against a curriculum's song list. No incumbent does this, because the curriculum is the incumbent's business.

What could kill it?

Pitch detection on a strummed six-string chord is genuinely hard, and the incumbents have had years of audio engineering on it; a thin wrapper over a generic pitch library will be heard as wrong within one session and never opened again.

Who pays, and for what?

Beginner guitarists are the primary buyers, seeking a tool that offers immediate feedback to accelerate their learning.

What is the main risk?

The risk lies in established platforms like Yousician, which already dominate the market with comprehensive music learning solutions.
