Product Hunt: Denovo — Turn your vibe-coded app into paying customers

Updated 2026-07-30 · Source https://www.producthunt.com/r/N632XKIQZO65KB?utm_campaign=producthunt… · 中文版

What is the idea?

A 'business manager' for indie app creators: monetization insights, pricing experiments, conversion tracking — the business-consultant layer for people who only know how to build.

Who would use it, and what do they get?

Indie creators are consumers here — they buy it like a course, not like SaaS.

Why would anyone bother?

Unlike generic analytics platforms, Denovo specifically caters to indie developers by focusing on monetization pain points and providing tailored solutions for converting users into revenue, without requiring deep business expertise.

What is missing today?

Vibe-coding produced a wave of builders with zero business skills, and tooling for 'make my toy app make money' is thin — but this entry needs first-hand evidence (a paying user, an earnest complaint) before promotion.

What makes this one different?

If evidence appears: the Chinese indie / 小程序 developer side is even more underserved than the English one.

What is the sharpest thing about it?

It sits where the money is decided rather than where the code is written: pricing, conversion and what to change next.

What could kill it?

App stores, Stripe and analytics platforms all sit closer to the money; 'insights' products die the moment the advice doesn't visibly move revenue.

Who pays, and for what?

Indie app creators pay for a tool that helps them convert free users into paying customers, enabling sustainable revenue growth without hiring a business consultant.

What is the main risk?

If major analytics platforms or app marketplaces improve their monetization tools, Denovo could struggle to differentiate itself and attract users.

How much work is it?

about two months of work
