Product Hunt: Totem — Your Twitter bookmarks organized to make you read them

Updated 2026-07-30 · Source https://www.producthunt.com/r/XYVSXCXMI3M4QN?utm_campaign=producthunt… · 中文版

What is the idea?

Totem organizes your Twitter bookmarks into a streamlined reading experience, allowing users to categorize and access saved tweets easily.

Who would use it, and what do they get?

Someone with two thousand saved posts they have never reopened. What they get is three of them, today, in a form they will actually read.

Why would anyone bother?

Users complain about the clutter of bookmarks and the difficulty in retrieving specific tweets; Totem addresses this by providing a clear organizational structure.

What is missing today?

Current solutions are either too technical for average users or lack a user-friendly interface, leaving a significant gap for a simple, effective tool.

What makes this one different?

I would focus on creating a visually appealing, user-friendly interface specifically designed for non-technical users, making it accessible for everyone.

What is the sharpest thing about it?

Bookmarks become a reading queue instead of a graveyard: saved posts are grouped and resurfaced so they get read.

What could kill it?

Twitter itself could easily implement a bookmarking feature that renders this tool redundant, as it already controls the user experience.

Who pays, and for what?

Casual Twitter users looking for an efficient way to manage and read their saved tweets.

What is the main risk?

Twitter's potential to integrate similar features directly into their platform poses a significant risk to the viability of this tool.

How much work is it?

about two weeks of work
