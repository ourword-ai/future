Estera

Estera — an AI receptionist that answers a business's phone calls and WhatsApp 24/7 (Product Hunt launch, 129 upvotes).

Updated 2026-07-28 · Source https://www.producthunt.com/r/GSPE3HHD2AHU47?utm_campaign=producthunt- · 中文版

What is the idea?

An AI receptionist that picks up a business's phone and WhatsApp around the clock, answers the standard questions, books the slot, and leaves the owner a summary in the morning. 129 Product Hunt upvotes on launch.

Who would use it, and what do they get?

Non-developer user: the owner of a one-person shop. Finished thing: the enquiries that came in after closing, answered before opening.

Why would anyone bother?

A single-chair salon, a two-bay garage, a solo dentist — the owner whose phone rings while both hands are busy. What they get the next morning is a list of who called, what each one wanted, and which one was worth calling back.

What is missing today?

The wedge is not the capability, it is the channel: every product in this category assumes a phone number and a calendar API. In the Chinese market the enquiry arrives as a WeChat message to a personal account, so the entire category is untranslatable as-is — and the Western version, meanwhile, is already crowded.

What makes this one different?

Do not build a receptionist; build the morning after. The owner forwards the night's unanswered messages into one place and gets back a ranked list — who asked what, what it is worth, one drafted reply each — which they send themselves in thirty seconds. No phone line, no calendar integration, no platform automation. Cold start (Chinese): 小红书 comment sections under 单人理发店 / 宠物店日常 posts where owners count the customers they lost; Cold start (English): r/smallbusiness, search 'missed calls after hours solo owner'.

What is the sharpest thing about it?

Holding a phone call convincingly in real time — sub-second latency, being interrupted, local accents — stopped being a research problem in roughly the last eighteen months. That is the entire reason this category exists in 2026 and did not in 2023.

What could kill it?

Voice receptionists are the single most contested lane in applied AI right now, funded from every direction, and the winners will be decided by latency and local-language reliability — which is a capital race, not a wedge. On the Chinese side, automating a personal WeChat account is against platform rules and this board's own integrity line.

Who pays, and for what?

A single-chair or single-bay owner, priced against one lost customer a month.

What is the main risk?

In English it is a crowded capital race; in Chinese the honest blocker is platform rules, and pretending otherwise is how this becomes parasitic.

How much work is it?

about two months of work
