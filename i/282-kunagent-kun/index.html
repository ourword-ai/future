KunAgent/Kun

Kun — an open agent workspace that chains requirement-clarification → UI design → code → writing into one loop, built to run well on cheap Chinese models (DeepSeek, Xiaomi MiMo, MiniMax).

Updated 2026-07-29 · Source https://github.com/KunAgent/Kun · 中文版

What is the idea?

Kun chains requirement-clarification → UI design → code → writing into one loop, so the handoffs between those four jobs happen inside one workspace.

Who would use it, and what do they get?

Partial — the promise is for non-engineers, but the current surface is an agent workspace, which is a developer artifact. The spec-interview slice is the part a normal person can actually take away.

Why would anyone bother?

Choose Kun for its demand-first coding paradigm that ensures your software development is driven by clear requirements, making it ideal for teams looking to enhance collaboration and efficiency.

What is missing today?

Chaining is not the scarce part; the scarce part is the first link — the clarifying interview that turns a vague want into a spec. Every tool in this space assumes you arrive with the spec already written.

What makes this one different?

Keep only the first link and sell it alone: a clarifying interview that ends with a one-page spec a normal person can hand to a human freelancer OR to any agent. Cold start CN = 小红书/即刻「找人做个小程序被坑」帖; EN = r/freelance and r/Upwork threads where clients arrive with no brief.

What is the sharpest thing about it?

Demand-clarification-first workflow plus first-class support for cost-efficient Chinese models, instead of a thin shell over one expensive API.

What could kill it?

Multi-stage agent workspaces are the most crowded category of 2026 and most die at the second handoff, where a small early misunderstanding compounds into an unusable artifact four steps later.

Who pays, and for what?

the wedge is the price-sensitive Chinese dev market — a hosted team edition priced against DeepSeek-level token costs, where US agent IDEs are expensive, blocked, or both.

What is the main risk?

Squeezed from both sides: DeepSeek/Xiaomi/MiniMax can ship native agent workspaces, and the clarification layer is copyable prompt-ware; kun-agent.com suggests a company is already forming around it — the opening may be theirs, not yours.

How much work is it?

about two months of work

Where does the evidence come from?

https://github.com/KunAgent/Kun
